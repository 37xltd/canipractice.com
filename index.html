Methodology

Clear answers.
Careful boundaries.

Can I Practice? joins official registers and guidance to answer one narrow question: whether a profession may be practised in a named jurisdiction. It does not assess your personal eligibility, visa, insurance, employment or competence.

Read the full coverage note ↗
01Identity firstWe resolve the exact profession, register and jurisdiction before making a claim.
02Freshness visibleEvery source carries an observation date. Old evidence is labelled, not quietly treated as current.
03Uncertainty earns a pauseWhere sources conflict or coverage is thin, the answer becomes “verify” — never a guess.
Coverage & limits

A useful first check,
not a legal opinion.

This pilot covers selected regulated professions and UK Crown Dependencies. Coverage expands only when we can identify a stable register, a lawful source and a meaningful user task.

Data is illustrative for this prototype. Always confirm with the regulator before starting work.
